# Trademark Policy

The names **SwarmNode**, **SwarmNode.space** and the project logo identify the official project and its devices.

## You may

- Refer to the project by name in articles, reviews, forks and academic work.
- State that your product is "compatible with SwarmNode" or "based on the SwarmNode specification", if true.
- Use the name to link to <https://swarmnode.space>.

## You may not

- Use the name or logo for a modified version in a way that implies it is official or endorsed.
- Register domains, social accounts or company names that are confusingly similar.
- Remove attribution from copies of the specification.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the repository licence. If you distribute a modified version, use a distinct name and do not present it as the official SwarmNode build.
